# Tabula Latina

A personal Latin trainer for the YouTube course
[Easy Latin Lessons](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLjqqMkeHFt1E_lRi9nQvtmP9Od2JokI1J),
published as a private claude.ai artifact.

- **Lessons**: every rule from the videos as What / How / When, the video's
  sentences and stories, with case endings in color.
- **Rules one at a time**: each video's rules in small parts (22 for Lessons
  I-IV). A part is explained very simply with examples, then two open questions
  are answered in your own words (checked by their key words, by Claude, or
  against a good answer). Only then do its exercises open; a rule you have not
  learned never appears in practice.
- **Train**: every word you know, only the rules you have learned. A new rule
  sinks in with simple exercises (choose the word, pick the meaning, build from
  tiles, type an ending in the gap); whole sentences to write and change come as
  it holds, and the next rule is offered when it does. Feedback names the case
  or person you used and the one needed, plus the rule behind it.
  Each exercise fits the window and says in a few plain words what to do; an
  ending or a form is typed right into the sentence; translations of Latin
  prompts stay hidden until you press Translate. Only sensible sentences.
- **Words**: your dictionary with spaced repetition; each word has its forms,
  sensible example sentences and, where one exists, a famous Latin saying.
- **Discover**: mystery cards, five at a time, each in its own fixed spot, with
  Latin words related to English, Russian or Ukrainian words; opened cards turn
  face up where they lie; add them and they join the exercises.
- **Rules**: every rule of the lessons you watched, one line each, with where you
  are on it (learned, 3/10, new); a tap opens it. Then the endings at a glance
  and the colors. The lessons' full rule texts wait under "More detail".
- **Audio**: every Latin sentence can be heard in classical pronunciation
  (through the device's Spanish or Italian voice), Listen & repeat on lesson pages, and a
  Listen and write exercise.
- **Sentences from the videos**: the sentences you heard, as exercises: choose
  the form, pick the meaning, build them from word tiles, write them.
- **Fābulae (short stories)**: twelve stories, three for each lesson, made only
  of the words from the videos; each opens when you have learned its rules. Tap a line for its
  English and a word for its meaning, listen, answer two questions, then practise
  the story's lines.
- **Fix my mistakes**: your mistakes come back later as new exercises on the
  same rules and words.
- **Latin in English**: what English took from Latin, from Roman traders to
  modern science; the English words that grew from each word you know; Latin
  verb forms, plurals, grammar rules and abbreviations inside English; and a
  "find the Latin" practice (dormitory → dormiō).
- **Ask Claude**: a tutor that sees your lessons, words and weak spots, and what
  is on your screen (the exercise, the lines of a lesson in view, the word or card
  you opened), so you can ask "what does this sentence mean?".
